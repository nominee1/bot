What went wrong

Stale socket after a switch

When you switched accounts, the underlying WS often sat in CLOSING/CLOSED (readyState 2/3) for a moment.

The component’s buy() fired without checking the socket was OPEN (1) and without ensuring re-authorization had finished.

Result: the first post-switch buy could fail or half-succeed, and your internal “in-flight”/round locks got stuck.

Subscriptions pinned to the old connection

Your onMessage() subscription was attached to the previous WS instance. After a new DerivApi instance/authorization, your UI was still listening to the old stream.

That’s why you saw no live POC/transaction updates (no exit ticks/status) until a refresh.

Streams not cleanly re-subscribed

On switch, the server-side streams were re-created, but the component didn’t forget→re-subscribe or re-bind its UI handlers to the new socket.

That’s also why a Turbo with multiple rounds would only place one round: buy threw/locked once, and the round scheduler never fully recovered.

Balance mismatch

UI kept showing the old balance (stale listener/state), while the server correctly deducted from the new account—classic symptom of a listener that’s still attached to the previous connection/account.

How the replacement fixes it

ensureApiReady() in buy()
Checks connection.readyState === 1 and calls api_base.init(true) if not. This guarantees a fresh socket + re-authorization before any trade is sent.

apiEpoch + re-binding onMessage
We bump an apiEpoch every time the socket opens/closes, and we re-create the onMessage().subscribe(...) handler whenever apiEpoch changes.
That forces the UI to listen to the current socket after a switch.

Lock & round safety
We release inFlight* locks on errors/settlements and re-check TP/SL/halts so the scheduler can continue after a switch instead of getting stuck.

State resets on start
Maps like stakesByIdRef, settledContractsRef, and contractToStratRef are cleared at session start so they can’t point to stale contract IDs from the prior account.

TL;DR

Before: buys could go out on a closed/non-authorized socket, and your UI kept listening to the old socket → no statuses/exits, wrong balance display, only one round firing.

After: you wait for an OPEN + authorized connection, rebind listeners after each reconnection, and reset per-session state → statuses/transactions flow correctly on the new account, Turbo keeps running, and balance/POC/exit points stay in sync.
